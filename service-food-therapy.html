Good to know

Common questions

So what exactly is a "food-therapy formula"?

A set of ordinary food ingredients, in set quantities, that you simmer together and drink. It's composed and cooked the way a Chinese herbal decoction is — the same logic, the same method — but built from foods rather than bitter herbs. What comes out is warm and concentrated, which is easy on a tired digestion, and tastes much closer to a good broth than to medicine.

How is this different from just the report?

The TCM Wellness Report tells you how your body's running, and gives you the everyday changes that stop the drain: sleep, warmth, how and what to eat and drink, movement. This adds the other half — a formula that gives a depleted system something to build back with.

Is it a meal plan?

No, and this is the thing worth being clear about. It's one formula you brew; it doesn't tell you what to have for lunch. If what you want is a look at how you actually eat day to day — your own habits, the timing, the temperature, the cooking — that's Food Therapy Coaching, which is where a genuine personalised food plan lives.

Do you sell or ship the ingredients?

No. We give you the formula and the method, and point you to trusted local Chinese grocers and regular supermarkets. Most of it is ordinary food. You buy it yourself.

How long does one formula last, and then what?

The formula is composed for how your body is running now. Bodies move — with the season, with sleep, with whatever life is doing. Most people fill the intake in again every month or so, so the doctor can see the current picture and adjust the formula to it.

Can I start with just the report and add this later?

You can, but the two are designed to go together, since a formula only makes sense once we understand your whole picture.
