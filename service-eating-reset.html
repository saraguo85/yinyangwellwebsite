Good to know

Common questions

Is this a diet or meal plan?

No. There are no strict rules or calorie counting. It's gentle, day-to-day guidance: small swaps, timing and cooking tweaks, matched to your body and easy to keep up.

Why don't you just send me the plan in week one?

Because a plan written from a questionnaire is a guess. Two weeks of your actual meals shows us what you really eat on a Tuesday night, what you reach for when you're busy, and what you already do well. A plan built on that is one you can keep; a plan built on assumptions is one you abandon.

Do I have to post every single meal?

In the first two weeks, post what you can — the more ordinary the better. Once your plan is written, we ask for all three meals a day plus a line on what you actually had, since that's the part the feedback works from.

Is it private?

Completely. Your group is just you and our team, no other clients. If you'd rather not show your face or full name, that's completely fine.

What if I have a medical condition or take medication?

Please tell us at the start, and keep seeing your GP. This is food and lifestyle guidance to support how you feel. It isn't medical treatment and doesn't replace your care.

Why a new report every month?

Because a read is of a body at a moment, not a permanent label. How you're running shifts with the season, with sleep, with whatever life is doing — and guidance built on a picture from six weeks ago is guidance for someone you no longer quite are. So each month begins with a fresh read, and the formula and the plan move with it.

How is this different from Report + Food Therapy Formula?

Report + Food Therapy Formula is a one-off: a read of how your body is running, and a formula to brew for it. Coaching includes both of those every month, and adds the part that's actually hard — changing how you eat day to day, with someone looking at your real meals and correcting as you go.

How often do you reply?

Twice a week. You post your meals as you eat them, and we come back on the run of them together rather than meal by meal — the useful signal is almost always the pattern, not the single plate. If something urgent comes up, say so in the group and we'll pick it up sooner.
