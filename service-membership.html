Good to know

Common questions

What's a "solar term"?

The Chinese calendar divides the year into 24 short seasons, about a fortnight each. TCM has always adjusted how you eat and live with them; membership gives you a guide for each.

How often will I hear from you?

Two seasonal guides a month, roughly one every fortnight, each with a Q&A window, plus the occasional members-only deep dive in between. Never overwhelming.

How do the members' questions work?

Everything happens by email. With each guide you can send us one question, simply by replying to the email it arrived in. We read every question and reply to every one. A selection go to a senior doctor at our partner clinic in China, and their answers appear in the next guide for all members. We choose the questions that apply to the most members, suit the season, and haven't been covered before. If your question is specific to you, we will answer the general version of it: what Traditional Chinese Medicine says about that situation in general, rather than what is happening in your body. For a read of your own body, that is what the TCM Wellness Report is for.

Can I cancel, and can I get a refund?

Cancel any time, no questions: your membership simply stops renewing. On monthly, if you're ever not happy, reply to any email and we'll refund your latest payment. On annual, you can have a full refund within 30 days of joining; after that it runs to the end of your year and won't renew once cancelled.

Is this medical advice?

No. It's seasonal lifestyle and food guidance from the TCM tradition, built around ingredients you can pick up in an ordinary supermarket. For a personalised read of your body, see the Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) Wellness Report.
