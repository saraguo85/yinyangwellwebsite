FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

Everything you might want to know before you start. Can't find it? Message us and we'll answer.

Is this medical advice?

No. YinYang Well offers lifestyle, dietary and wellness guidance based on Traditional Chinese Medicine. It isn't medical diagnosis or treatment and doesn't replace your GP or specialist. If you have a medical condition or worrying symptoms, please see a licensed healthcare provider. And never stop prescribed medication without your doctor.

Who actually reviews my report?

Every Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) Wellness Report and food-therapy plan is read and signed off by a senior TCM doctor at our partner clinic in Ningbo, China. These are doctors with decades of clinical experience. It's never auto-generated and left unchecked.

Do I need to be in the UK or China?

Neither. It's all remote: you fill in your intake online from anywhere, and your plan comes back to you digitally.

Which photos do you ask for, and why?

Nine in all: your tongue, face, palms, backs of the hands, nails and both ears. In Traditional Chinese Medicine these are the clearest windows into how your body's running — warmth, damp, circulation. Taking them on your phone in natural light is enough, and it lets the doctor read your whole picture far more accurately.

What's the difference between the £69 and £149 options?

The £69 TCM Wellness Report tells you how your body's running and what's driving it, and gives you the everyday changes that shift it: sleep, warmth, how and what to eat and drink, movement. The £149 option adds a food-therapy formula on top: a brew you simmer at home, made the way a Chinese herbal decoction is but from everyday foods, put together for what your report found. Because a formula only makes sense once we understand how your body's running, it always includes the report. Neither is a meal plan — for a look at how you actually eat day to day, that's Food Therapy Coaching.

How and when do I get my plan?

Once your intake is reviewed (usually within five working days), your plan is delivered digitally and we'll set you up with a private channel, typically a WhatsApp thread or group, where you can read it and ask follow-up questions.

How does payment work?

Securely online via Stripe: card or the usual digital wallets. One-off services are paid upfront; the membership is a simple monthly or yearly subscription you can cancel anytime.

Is Traditional Chinese Medicine safe alongside my normal healthcare?

The guidance we give is about food and lifestyle, designed to sit alongside conventional care, not replace it. Always keep your GP in the loop, keep taking prescribed medication, and tell us about any conditions so we can guide you sensibly.

Can I get a refund?

Because reports involve a doctor's time and are personalised to you, they can't be refunded once the review has started. If something's gone wrong, message us. We'll always try to make it right.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp and a real person (or, soon, our friendly assistant) will help.
